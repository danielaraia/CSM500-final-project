# Stage 5 update

The first results used one seed and they were produced before some pipeline fixes. They are kept as exploratory work but they are not the final result.

The corrected grid finished with 169 valid runs. Six patients produced scores. Sub-073 had no test windows after the boundary purging and it was excluded.

At SOP 30, the mean sensitivity was 0.667 for Pearson, 0.417 for mutual information and 0.500 for attention. Their false alarm rates were 0.231, 0.121 and 0.198 per hour. The intervals are wide, so no graph method is a clear winner. Full results are in `results/experiment-analysis.json`.
